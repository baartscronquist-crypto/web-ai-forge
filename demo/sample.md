# A small greenhouse irrigation trial

This fictional trial compares how much water a drip line delivers to six trays.

## Methods

After a period of ten minutes of watering, measurements of the water delivered to each of the six trays were made to make the comparison possible. Volumes are reported in milliliters.

For tray $i$, the mean delivery rate $q_i$ is the collected volume $V_i$ divided by the watering time $\Delta t$.

$$
q_i = \frac{V_i}{\Delta t}
$$
